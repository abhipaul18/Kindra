About this task

Join us for a morning of community building and environmental stewardship at Centennial Park. We will be clearing fallen branches, picking up litter, and preparing the soil for new native plant installations next month.

This initiative is crucial for maintaining the park's ecosystem and ensuring it remains a safe, beautiful space for all residents. Your participation directly contributes to the local community's well-being and earns you civic karma points.

What to bring:

  • Comfortable, closed-toe shoes
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Sunscreen and a hat
  • Gardening gloves (if you have them, otherwise provided)
