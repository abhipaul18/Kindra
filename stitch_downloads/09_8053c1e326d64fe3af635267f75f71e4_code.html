The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-Kisan) scheme is a central sector scheme that provides income support to all landholding farmers' families in the country to supplement their financial needs for procuring various inputs related to agriculture and allied activities as well as domestic needs.

Under the scheme, a financial benefit of ₹6,000 per year is provided to eligible beneficiary farmer families, payable in three equal four-monthly installments of ₹2,000 each.
